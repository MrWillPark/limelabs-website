How It Works for Customers
  1. 01
    AI Photo Identification

    Users snap a photo of any plant in their home to instantly identify the species and get Oakland-calibrated care schedules.

  2. 02
    Smart Care Reminders

    The app sends gentle notifications when it’s time to water, mist, or fertilize so plants stay thriving in East Bay microclimates.

  3. 03
    AI-assisted Photo Diagnosis

    Yellow leaf or pests? One photo diagnoses the issue (spider mites, root rot) and gives an instant step-by-step treatment plan.

  4. 04
    Direct In-Store Pickup

    The diagnosis tells customers the exact remedy they need (neem oil, aroid soil, pots) with 1-tap pickup at 1700 Center Street.

The Partnership Model
Digital Subscriptions 10% to BGGH

Every subscriber ($4.99/mo) earns BGGH 10% monthly recurring software revenue — whether they adopted in-store, clicked a bio link, or found the app online.

Soil, Neem & Pot Sales 90% to BGGH

When the app recommends remedies and supplies, local users buy directly from your shop. BGGH keeps 90% of the retail price.

Web App or Native iOS Flexible Reach

Shareable everywhere: a link on @blkgirls_greenhouse, bggh.shop, or a dedicated App Store download.

Zero Tech Costs Lime Labs Backing

$0 setup fee, $0 monthly fee, and $0 ongoing maintenance. Lime Labs covers 100% of servers, computer vision AI recognition models, App Store approvals, and customer bug support. BGGH never touches code or server bills.
